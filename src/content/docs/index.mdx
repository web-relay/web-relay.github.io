## Actions should travel with you.

A note app knows how to create a note. A workspace extension knows how to restore your tabs. The browser knows the page you are on. Today, those actions live in separate menus. Web Relay proposes a capability registry that brings them together.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Local by design" icon="laptop">Command execution should work inside your browser and applications. No account, server, or cloud dependency is required for the MVP.</Card>
  <Card title="Define once" icon="forward-slash">Register a capability once. Reuse it in your app’s palette and the browser launcher, with agents and voice as future interfaces.</Card>
  <Card title="Context comes along" icon="puzzle">Show relevant actions for the active app, route, and state. Avoid asking for the customer, repository, or episode your app already knows.</Card>
  <Card title="Start with what you own" icon="seti:config">Focus on personal PWAs, your extensions, and internal tools. Prove useful integrations before considering a catalogue or marketplace.</Card>
</CardGrid>
